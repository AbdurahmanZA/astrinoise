  • *7866 and talk: the echo test plays your own voice back cleaned. Compare with *43.
  • Works whatever the switch above says; it only affects your own test call.

What it does

  • Cleans the agent's microphone (fans, hum, keyboards, background voices) on calls that go out through a trunk: desk phones and softphones. Internal calls are never touched.
  • Never on an emergency route.
  • Adds a fixed delay (RNNoise about 50 ms, DeepFilterNet a little more); the customer's voice is untouched.
  • Recordings of outbound calls keep the agent's original voice.
  • Off stops it for every new call at once.
